About

Have a peek at what I love to do.

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at VIT Bhopal with a design practice that sits between research and visual craft — I care as much about why a flow works as how it looks.

My experience sits at the intersection of UI/UX design, visual storytelling, and technology. From Adobe Express brand work to Figma prototyping for e-commerce and mobile products, I approach design with a focus on understanding user needs, building thoughtful flows, and creating intuitive digital experiences. My background in IT support has further developed my problem-solving skills and given me insight into the practical challenges users face when interacting with technology. Outside of work, my experience leading my university's Anime Club and participating in drama continues to influence how I think about pacing, tone, and audience experience.
